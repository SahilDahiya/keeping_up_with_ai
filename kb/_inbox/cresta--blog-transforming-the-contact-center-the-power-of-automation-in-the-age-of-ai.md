# Contact Center Automation in the Age of AI | Cresta

The modern contact center is the frontline of customer experience, a critical touchpoint that can make or break brand loyalty. Yet, despite its importance, many contact centers are struggling to keep up with the rising tide of customer expectations.The culprit? Outdated processes, siloed systems, and the inefficiencies that come with them. Fortunately, contact center automation can free businesses from these barriers, enabling them to transform contact centers into brand-building powerhouses they’re meant to be.

## The current state of contact centers

Before diving into the transformative potential of contact center automation, it’s crucial to understand the current landscape. Traditionally, many contact centers have relied on manual processes, from routing calls to monitoring agent performance. These manual methods are not only time-consuming but also prone to human error, leading to inconsistent customer experiences and high operational costs.With manual processes remaining so prominent, it’s little wonder that automating manual activities in the contact center is [cited as a top investment area](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/the-state-of-customer-care-in-2022) for businesses looking to enhance customer care. As customer demands evolve—expecting faster, more personalized service—the traditional, manual methods are proving inadequate.

## The promise of contact center automation

Automation in the contact center is about more than just streamlining processes; it’s about fundamentally rethinking how customer service is delivered. By automating routine tasks such as call routing, data entry, and performance monitoring, businesses can free up their agents to focus on more complex, value-added interactions, leading to benefits like:

- **Enhanced customer experience:** One of the most significant benefits of contact center automation is the ability to provide a more consistent and personalized customer experience. Automated systems can quickly analyze customer data, predict needs, and offer tailored solutions in real time.For example,[AI-powered agents](https://cresta.com/blog/announcing-cresta-voice-virtual-agent-what-sets-it-apart-what-the-market-is-getting-wrong/) can handle simple inquiries 24/7, ensuring that customers receive prompt responses, even outside of business hours.
- **Improved operational efficiency:** Automation reduces the time agents spend on repetitive tasks, allowing them to handle more calls and resolve issues faster. With more than[60% of contact centers](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/us/Documents/process-and-operations/us-consulting-trends-and-ai-in-contact-center.pdf) reporting that they’re currently facing a staffing shortage, it’s crucial to empower agents to work more efficiently.
- **Data-driven insights:** With automation, contact centers can harness the power of big data. Automated systems can analyze large volumes of customer interactions, providing insights into common issues, customer sentiment, and agent performance. These insights can then be used to refine processes, improve training, and optimize the overall customer experience.

## The role of AI in contact center automation

AI is the engine driving the next wave of contact center automation. Generative AI, in particular, opens up new possibilities for enhancing customer interactions and improving operational efficiency. Contact center automation powered by AI may feature more advanced capabilities, such as:

- **Smart routing:** AI-powered systems can analyze incoming calls or messages and route them to the most appropriate agent based on the customer’s history, preferences, and the nature of the inquiry. This reduces the time customers spend on hold and ensures they are connected to someone who can resolve their issue quickly.
- **Predictive analytics:** AI can predict customer behavior based on past interactions and real-time data, enabling contact centers to anticipate customer needs and proactively address issues before they escalate. This not only improves customer satisfaction but also reduces the volume of incoming calls.
- **Agent assistance:** Generative AI can assist agents during calls by providing real-time suggestions and proactively surfacing information, helping them navigate complex inquiries and ensuring that they adhere to compliance requirements. This leads to faster resolution times and a more consistent customer experience.
- **Performance management:** AI-driven automation can continuously monitor and evaluate agent performance, providing managers with actionable insights. This allows for more targeted coaching and development, ensuring that agents are always performing at their best.

## Calculating the human element: Balancing automation with empathy

While contact center automation offers many benefits, it’s essential to remember the true value of automation comes from empowering human agents to fulfill their highest, best purpose. Customers still value empathy and personal connection, particularly in situations involving complex or sensitive issues. In turn, the goal of automation should not be to replace human agents but to empower them to deliver better service.By automating routine tasks, agents are free to focus on building rapport with customers and handling more nuanced inquiries. Plus, the insights generated by automation solutions can help agents better understand customer needs, allowing them to provide more empathetic and effective service.

## Looking ahead: An augmented future with contact center automation

As AI and automation technologies continue to evolve, the contact center of the future will look very different from today. We can expect to see even greater integration of AI, with systems becoming more adept at handling complex inquiries and providing personalized service at scale, enabling contact centers to embrace more proactive customer service.Companies that successfully integrate automation into their contact centers will be those that take a holistic approach, combining technology with a strong focus on the customer experience and the development of their human agents.By embracing automation in conjunction with AI, contact centers can deliver faster, more personalized service, improve operational efficiency, and gain valuable insights into customer behavior.As businesses look to the future, contact center automation will undoubtedly play a critical role in shaping the customer experience, driving operational excellence, and maintaining a competitive edge.
