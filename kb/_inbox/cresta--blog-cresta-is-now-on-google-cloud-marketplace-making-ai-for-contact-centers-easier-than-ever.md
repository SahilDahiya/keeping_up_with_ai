# Cresta Is Now on Google Cloud Marketplace

We’ve got some exciting news to share! Cresta is now officially available on the [**Google Cloud Marketplace**](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/cresta-public/cresta) with deployment options on Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

This marks a big step forward in our mission to turn every customer conversation into a competitive advantage by unlocking the true potential of the contact center. Through our expanded partnership with Google Cloud, companies can now access the full breadth of Cresta’s offerings—seamlessly deployed on Google Cloud’s trusted infrastructure—making it easier to adopt, scale, and drive value with our end-to-end AI platform.

## Why This Matters

At Cresta, we believe AI should work with and for people, not the other way around. Our platform helps contact centers unlock valuable insights, automate time-consuming tasks, and empower teams with real-time support, ensuring that every conversation drives better outcomes.

With our listing on Google Cloud Marketplace, more businesses can now:

- **Discover Cresta faster:** You can find and procure our solutions directly through the Marketplace, using your existing vendor relationships.
- **Deploy with flexibility:** Already running on Google Cloud? Great: you can now seamlessly deploy Cresta within your current infrastructure, scaling to meet your unique needs.
- **Make smarter financial decisions:** Google Cloud customers can apply their committed cloud spend toward Cresta, making it easier to get started and manage costs through unified billing.
- **Trust in our security:** Cresta is the first contact center AI provider to achieve[ISO/IEC 42001 certification](https://cresta.com/blog/cresta-secures-first-iso-42001-certification-in-contact-center-ai-space/) , so you can count on robust security and compliance standards from day one.

## What This Means for Our Customers

This milestone follows a wave of innovation from our team, including updates on [Cresta AI Agent](https://cresta.com/blog/how-brinks-home-is-redefining-automation-with-cresta-ai-agent/) and the release of [Cresta AI Analyst](https://cresta.com/blog/introducing-ai-analyst-business-insights-delivered-in-minutes/), which are already helping top brands like [Brinks](https://cresta.com/blog/our-own-zero-to-one-lessons-learned-in-building-the-brinks-home-ai-agent/), [Snap Finance](https://cresta.com/customers/snap-finance/), and more enhance the customer experience, save time and costs, and drive revenue growth.

Here’s how our VP of Partnerships, **Lukasz Szostak**, put it:“We’re bringing Cresta to Google Cloud Marketplace to democratize access and simplify the deployment of next-generation AI for contact centers. This allows us to streamline the procurement process for customers and enable organizations to more easily deploy our solutions—accelerating their AI transformation journey.

”We also heard from **Dai Vu**, Managing Director at Google Cloud:“Bringing Cresta to Google Cloud Marketplace will help customers quickly deploy, manage, and grow Cresta’s contact center solutions on Google Cloud’s trusted, global infrastructure.”

## Want to Learn More?

If you’re already a Google Cloud customer, it’s now easier than ever to bring the power of Cresta into your contact center: just head to the [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/cresta-public/cresta) to explore our solutions.

Here’s to turning every conversation into a competitive advantage and delighting customers — powered by AI and made even more accessible through Google Cloud.
