# Welcoming Dannie Herzberg to Baseten

Baseten welcomes Dannie Herzberg to Baseten as our new President to lead GTM and Operations.


- Authors
- Date
- August 27, 2025

TLDR

Dannie Herzberg has joined Baseten as our new President to lead the GTM and operations. Dannie brings a rare combination of operational excellence and customer-centric vision from her experience spanning HubSpot, Slack, and Sequoia. Her track record of scaling world-class companies will accelerate our mission to deliver the fastest, most reliable inference.

At Baseten, our promise to customers is simple: deliver inference with the fastest speed, highest reliability, and best developer experience. This only works if every part of the company is aligned around making customers successful.

Today, I’m thrilled to announce that Dannie Herzberg is joining us as President to lead our go-to-market and operations.

At HubSpot, Dannie helped shape the go-to-market organization as an early employee. At Slack, she saw scale firsthand as the company grew from $100m to over $1B in revenue leading up to its IPO. Most recently, at Sequoia, she saw greatness working alongside founders building the most enduring companies. This rare combination of experience and perspective makes her an exceptional fit for Baseten.

In our search for the right leader, we wanted someone who reflects the core of our product and brand: speed, reliability, and a deep commitment to customer success.

Dannie embodies all three. She is biased towards action, dependable, and long-term in her thinking, and she knows that the customer experience is everything.

AI is the most significant economic opportunity of our lifetimes. It’s moving faster than any of us can fully grasp, and companies need to respond with discipline and speed. With Dannie’s leadership, we’re better positioned than ever to deliver on our mission and to serve our customers.

Welcome, Dannie.
