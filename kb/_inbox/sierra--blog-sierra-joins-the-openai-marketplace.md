# Sierra joins the OpenAI Marketplace

We’re excited to be a launch partner in [OpenAI’s new marketplace](https://openai.com/business/marketplace/). Now, eligible customers can build agents with Sierra using their OpenAI commitments — helping them to build better customer experiences and drive measurable business outcomes, like refinance a loan, prevent a subscriber from churning or completing a sale, using their existing budget.

## **Why Sierra?**

Almost 50% of the Fortune 50 work with Sierra, as well as 1 in 3 of the leading banks, and 5 out of 10 of the largest healthcare companies in the world. Why?

- **[Pay for outcomes, not tokens](https://sierra.ai/blog/outcomemaxxing):** A loan refinanced, an insurance claim paid, a sale closed, or a customer retained.
- **[Easy to use](https://sierra.ai/blog/ghostwriter-ai-tool-to-teammate):** With Ghostwriter — our agent for building agents — anyone on your team can build, deploy and improve agents.
- **[Highly adaptable](https://sierra.ai/blog/context-engineering-the-key-to-great-agents):** Most agent builders use one-size-fits all SOPs that struggle with harder use cases. Sierra’s goals and guardrails enable agents to reason through the toughest issues and grow with your ambitions.
- **[Visibility and control](https://sierra.ai/blog/your-agent-laid-bare-and-why-it-matters):** With Sierra you can understand how your agent works, integrate it with existing systems, and make changes directly.
- **[Industry expertise](https://x.com/btaylor/status/2084638932025975027):** Sierra’s agent development team has built thousands of agents across industries. Their knowledge helps you deploy in weeks not months.
- [**From cost control to growth**](https://sierra.ai/blog/context-engine) : Use the context of customer conversations to differentiate your business and build a durable competitive moat.

This announcement builds on our existing work with OpenAI, including Sierra’s participation in [OpenAI Frontier](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-frontier/). As a launch partner in the OpenAI B2B marketplace, we can now reach a wider pool of enterprise customers looking to apply a portion of their OpenAI commitment toward building with Sierra.

If you’d like to partner with us through the OpenAI marketplace, reach out to **oai-marketplace@sierra.ai**.
