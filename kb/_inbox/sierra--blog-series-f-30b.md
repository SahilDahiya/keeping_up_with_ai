# Series F (Building the digital front door)

# Building the digital front door

We’re raising $1.75 billion at a valuation of $30 billion, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners. It’s especially exciting that all our previous lead investors — Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks, GV, and Tiger Global — are doubling down on Sierra.

## AI “dog years”

Artificial intelligence is turning the old adage that we overestimate the impact of technology in the short term and underestimate it in the long term on its head.

In November 2022, few people believed ChatGPT, a research project, would upend our perceptions of technology overnight. Or that in 12 months Silicon Valley would go from writing code by hand to AI doing the coding. Or that last week, a five year old AI lab would pull off the biggest IPO in history. Such is the speed at which AI is moving, and the value it has created.

## AI beyond the Valley

Alongside this excitement, there’s a lot of anxiety, including that this is another bubble — that the enormous sums being invested in AI won’t deliver an adequate return. Our experience tells a different story. We’re seeing some of the oldest, most regulated companies in the world deploy AI the fastest — deepening their customer relationships, generating efficiencies, and driving growth.

It’s mind boggling to be working with businesses whose roots date back to the nineteenth century or earlier — [BNY](https://x.com/SierraPlatform/status/2112216479689253285) (1784), [Cigna](https://sierra.ai/customers/the-cigna-group) (1792), [JPMorganChase](https://sierra.ai/customers/jpmorganchase) (1799), [Pearson](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7458268433286197248/) (1844), [Wells Fargo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yejeQG2hnA4) (1852), [Santander](https://x.com/AnaBotin/status/2077392299860926837) (1857), [Sutter Health](https://sierra.ai/customers/sutter-health) (1858), [Guardian](https://sierra.ai/customers/guardian) (1860), [Next](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brettaylor_how-next-transforms-global-customer-service-activity-7432479457241681920-j-9Y) (1864), [HSBC](https://sierra.ai/uk/sierra-summit-2026-london-register) (1865), [ScottsMiracle-Gro](https://sierra.ai/customers/scottsmiraclegro) (1868), [ADT](https://sierra.ai/customers/adt) (1874), [Prudential Financial](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7440820554510516224/) (1875), [BISSELL](https://www.fastcompany.com/91598417/pacesetters-2026) (1876), [Singtel](https://sierra.ai/customers/singtel) (1879), and [Cox Automotive](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/justin-leamer-5670b352_this-week-our-team-at-cox-automotive-inc-share-7420573732445790208-qFPV/) (1898). These businesses, and the [hundreds of others](https://sierra.ai/customers) we work with across every industry, are the pioneers of applied AI and we’re so proud to partner with them.

## Your digital front door

We founded Sierra in the belief that AI agents would become the digital front door for every company, deepening their relationships with customers and opening up new growth opportunities. And we’ve become the leader in this space — reaching [over $400M in ARR](https://sierra.ai/blog/our-customers-ambition-is-accelerating-our-growth) in just over two and a half years — for three primary reasons.

- ***We [charge for outcomes](https://sierra.ai/blog/outcomemaxxing), not usage or tokens*** — a problem resolved, a loan originated, a driver onboarded, an insurance claim paid, a subscriber saved. This keeps our interests aligned and Sierra focused on delivering measurable business impact for your company.
- ***Our platform enables any team to build sophisticated agents*** — you define the goals and guardrails and [Ghostwriter](https://sierra.ai/blog/ghostwriter-ai-tool-to-teammate) (our AI to build AI) will build, test, and deploy your agents. It also improves them over time: reviewing conversations, spotting opportunities, and suggesting new experiments just like your best teammates.
- ***Our partnership model is highly adaptable*** — because every enterprise is different. Your company’s deep domain expertise combined with our experience deploying AI at scale means agents can go live in weeks. And our [constellation of models](https://sierra.ai/blog/constellation-of-models) keeps businesses at the frontier without you having to worry about which model to use or your token spend.

If you’re interested in working with Sierra, we’d love to hear from you.
