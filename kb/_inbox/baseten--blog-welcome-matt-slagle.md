# Welcome, Matt Slagle!

We are thrilled to announce that Matt Slagle has joined Baseten to build and scale our global revenue organization.


- Authors
- Date
- March 12, 2026


At Baseten, our ambition is to power the most important AI products in the world. Doing that requires not just great technology, but a customer organization that can partner deeply with teams building at the frontier of AI.


Matt brings deep experience building and scaling high-performance sales teams. Most recently, he served as SVP of Sales at Sigma. Before that, he spent more than nine years in sales leadership roles at Fauna and New Relic during periods of rapid growth. In each chapter, he built deeply technical and customer-centric teams.


In our search for this role, we wanted someone who could architect a modern customer organization from first principles — and someone who reflects the traits we value most at Baseten: integrity, intensity, low ego, and deep pride in the craft.


Matt embodies all of these, and we’re very glad he’s here.


Welcome, Matt.
