# Baseten partners with Google Cloud to deliver high-performance AI infrastructure to a broader audience

Baseten is now on Google Cloud Marketplace, empowering organizations with the tools to build and scale AI applications effortlessly.


- Authors
- Date
- September 26, 2024

TLDR

We're excited to announce our availability on the Google Cloud Marketplace, a significant step in our mission to empower organizations with the tools they need to build and scale AI applications effortlessly. This partnership enables Google Cloud customers to access Baseten’s robust AI infrastructure platform with just a few clicks, making it easier than ever to deploy, manage, and optimize AI models in their existing cloud environments.

With our new integration on the [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/baseten-public/baseten?project=baseten-public), Google Cloud users can now leverage Baseten’s platform directly within their cloud ecosystem, benefiting from seamless interoperability, secure data management, and the ability to quickly spin up high-performance AI applications. For companies already relying on Google Cloud’s extensive suite of services, Baseten offers a powerful, complementary solution that enhances their AI capabilities without adding operational complexity.

Baseten’s availability on Google Cloud Marketplace allows us to deliver even more value to our customers. We’re helping enterprises overcome the scaling challenges that come with AI inference and enabling them to optimize their existing infrastructure for the best performance, security, and efficiency.


This partnership addresses a key demand in the market: the need for scalable, reliable AI infrastructure that integrates smoothly with existing cloud solutions. Whether you’re a startup experimenting with new AI models or an enterprise scaling mission-critical applications, Baseten on Google Cloud ensures that you have the infrastructure and support to achieve your goals efficiently.

Google Cloud customers can now enjoy the ease of deploying Baseten’s platform with the added assurance of Google Cloud’s robust security and compliance standards. This integration simplifies the AI deployment process, allowing teams to focus on innovation and performance rather than infrastructure management. From automated scaling to integrated monitoring and analytics, Baseten’s platform brings a new level of sophistication to AI workflows on Google Cloud.

The Google Cloud Marketplace offers a streamlined procurement process, allowing customers to purchase and manage software with a few clicks, reducing procurement friction and enabling faster time-to-value. With Baseten available on the Marketplace, organizations can rapidly onboard our platform, benefit from unified billing, and leverage existing cloud credits.

Bringing Baseten’s Hybrid Mode to Google Cloud Marketplace will help customers quickly deploy, manage, and grow their AI infrastructure solution on Google Cloud's trusted, global infrastructure. Baseten can now securely scale and support customers on their digital transformation journeys.


This partnership also underscores our commitment to making AI accessible and manageable for every organization, regardless of their size or complexity. By partnering with Google Cloud, we are providing a trusted and proven pathway for businesses to adopt high-performance AI infrastructure tailored to their unique needs.

While our Google Cloud partnership is the highlight, we’re also offering early access to our [Hybrid Mode](https://www.baseten.co/solutions/baseten-hybrid/), designed for organizations that require the flexibility to run AI workloads across both cloud and on-premises environments. Hybrid Mode empowers businesses to optimize performance and cost, leveraging Google Cloud for scalability while maintaining on-premises infrastructure for data locality and compliance.

Hybrid Mode was developed specifically in response to the needs of our customers running large-scale AI inference. It empowers them to balance security, performance, and cost-effectiveness by seamlessly moving workloads between Google Cloud and Baseten’s cloud infrastructure. This new offering expands the flexibility of how our customers can scale their AI workloads.


As Baseten and Google Cloud continue to collaborate, we’re committed to delivering even more integrated solutions that push the boundaries of what’s possible with AI. Our shared vision is to provide every organization with the tools they need to build, deploy, and scale AI models effortlessly, unlocking new opportunities for innovation and growth.

Whether you’re looking to accelerate your AI initiatives or enhance your existing capabilities, Baseten on Google Cloud is your partner in building the future of intelligent applications.

Explore our offering on the [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/baseten-public/baseten?project=baseten-public) or [reach out to us](https://www.baseten.co/talk-to-us/fall-into-inference/) to learn how Baseten can help you take your AI projects to the next level.
