# Closing QM Gaps with Screen Recording in Contact Centers

As the world evolves, so have contact centers — along with the technology underpinning their operations. By [embracing modern tech](https://cresta.com/blog/the-role-of-people-in-ai-maturity-building-a-culture-of-ai-readiness-in-your-contact-center/), contact centers can position themselves to provide superior customer experiences, streamline interactions, boost efficiency, and more.

The only problem is that growth is often incremental. Just like a quilt pieced together over time, most contact centers have gradually invested in disparate solutions, leaving them with a piecemeal tech stack with individual tools for call recording, coaching, QA workflows, and beyond.

Cresta has long pursued the goal of providing and optimizing an end-to-end solution for contact center operations. Screen recording is the proverbial “last mile” to achieving comprehensive visibility into agent interactions for unparalleled quality management. With our new screen recording feature, Cresta offers a truly holistic solution for [conversation intelligence](https://cresta.com/conversation-intelligence/), [agent assist](https://cresta.com/cresta-agent-assist/), and [quality management](https://cresta.com/conversation-intelligence/quality-management) in a unified platform.

So, what exactly makes screen recording such an important innovation for contact centers? And just as importantly, how can agents be sure this feature isn’t cause for concern? We’ll explore all of this and more.

## Unpacking the importance of streamlined screen recording in contact centers

In today’s customer service environment, robust compliance strategies and performance management are non-negotiable. Contact centers are the lifeline of customer engagement, handling sensitive data, processing transactions, and navigating complex interactions daily. Without screen recording, contact center leaders — particularly those tasked with safeguarding quality and compliance — lack complete visibility into customer interactions.

Still, fragmented point solutions — which have long been the only way leaders could gain a composite view of interactions to ensure compliance, aid in dispute resolution, and streamline QA — cost valuable time. On average, [people spend 36 minutes every day](https://assets.qatalog.com/language.work/qatalog-2021-workgeist-report.pdf) simply switching between applications. At scale, this problem quickly becomes untenable.It’s clear that contact centers need screen recording to effectively address their quality management and compliance needs, but it’s also clear they need those capabilities to exist within a holistic solution, rather than as a satellite tool that requires additional time and budget.

## Compliance, QA, CX, and beyond: The benefits of contact center screen recording

While screen recording may seem like a simple addition to contact operations, the benefits are far-reaching. Some of the most impactful results include:

- **Compliance with regulatory standards
** Many contact centers operate in highly regulated industries and handle sensitive data such as payment information and personal identification. Meanwhile, regulations like PCI-DSS, GDPR, and HIPAA require strict adherence to data privacy standards. Screen recording enables verifiable compliance by providing a detailed record of agent actions during transactions, ensuring transparency and accountability.
- **Real-time collaboration**
With Cresta’s Live Assist feature, both managers and agents can establish a live chat session - agents by ‘raising their hand’ to get real-time help during a live call, and managers through sending messages. Coupled with Live Streaming, managers are also able to see from the agent’s perspective - and guide them more effectively through live support/coaching and performance improvement. Live Streaming enables contact center leaders to view an agent’s desktop and access audio and transcription together during a live conversation, helping to ensure real-time compliance and in-the-moment assistance.
- **Quality assurance and performance improvement
** Screen recording provides another layer of actionable insights for coaching and performance management. Supervisors can review both the agent’s verbal communication and their corresponding digital workflow, identifying bottlenecks, training needs, or process inefficiencies. Over time, this leads to consistent service improvements and higher customer satisfaction.
- **Enhanced customer experience
** By analyzing screen recordings, contact centers can identify pain points in customer interactions, such as long hold times or errors in data entry. These insights could help refine workflows, enabling faster resolution times and more accurate service.
- **Data-driven decision making
** With screen recording, organizations gain a wealth of data to identify trends and inefficiencies. These recordings can uncover recurring challenges in tools or workflows, empowering IT teams to implement targeted solutions and automation opportunities.

## Why screen recording is an agent’s right-hand man — not “Big Brother”

A common misconception about screen recording is that it has the potential to be used as a surveillance tool aimed at micromanaging agents. However, this couldn’t be further from the truth. Far from a tool for “spying,” properly implemented screen recording solutions foster a culture of accountability and mutual benefit.

#### Focused on compliance, not control

Screen recording ensures agents and businesses remain compliant with laws and regulations, protecting both parties from legal risks. For agents, it’s a safety net that ensures they’re supported in following correct procedures.

#### Used to improve, not penalize

Rather than being a punitive tool, screen recording enables even more personalized coaching. By analyzing specific interactions with visibility into on-screen activity, managers can provide constructive feedback tailored to each individual agent’s workflow and challenges, fostering professional growth.

#### Aiding in resolution, not oversight

When disputes or legal inquiries arise, screen recording solutions provide corroboration that agents performed their roles effectively and in compliance with relevant regulations, alleviating stress over potential disputes.

## Building a foundation for workflow automation

Screen recording doesn’t just address compliance and quality assurance — it also sets the stage for workflow automation. By identifying repetitive tasks and inefficient processes, contact centers can streamline operations and reduce agent workloads.

For instance, screen recordings may reveal that agents spend significant time manually entering customer information. AI-driven tools automate this task, freeing agents to focus on resolving customer issues.

What’s more, by reviewing patterns in screen recordings, contact centers can uncover discrepancies and standardize workflows to eliminate unnecessary steps and optimize operations broadly. When implemented properly, screen recording can be leveraged as a cornerstone of the broader digital transformation in contact centers, driving efficiency, innovation, and satisfaction.
