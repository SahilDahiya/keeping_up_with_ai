# What Is Contact Center Speech Analytics?

As businesses look for novel ways to stand apart and get ahead, it’s little surprise that [85% of decision-makers](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/align-your-sales-and-service-teams/) are looking to their service teams to contribute a larger share of revenue this year. But how exactly can contact centers live up to this expectation, enhancing interactions with customers while pursuing more targeted business outcomes? One foundational technology that cannot be overlooked is contact center speech analytics. By providing deep insights into customer interactions, these solutions help to optimize agent performance and enable tighter alignment to goals.We’ll delve into what contact center speech analytics is, how it brings value to businesses, and key considerations for choosing the right solution.

## Understanding contact center speech analytics

Contact center speech analytics involves the use of sophisticated technology to analyze customer interactions in real time or post call. This analysis is performed on voice data captured during customer calls, aiming to extract valuable insights from the conversation.By employing natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms, these solutions can identify patterns, emotions, and key phrases that provide a deeper understanding of customer sentiment and behavior. Advanced solutions can even transform conversational data into key business insights.

## Why leverage speech analytics in contact centers?

Implementing contact center speech analytics solutions can revolutionize the way businesses handle customer interactions. For example, these solutions support goals like:

1. **Enhancing customer experience:** By analyzing customer interactions, businesses can better identify pain points and areas for improvement in their service delivery. Understanding customer sentiment helps agents tailor responses that lead to higher satisfaction rates.
2. **Agent performance management:** Speech analytics solutions provide insights into agent performance by evaluating their communication skills, adherence to scripts, and ability to handle customer queries. This data can be used to provide targeted training and coaching, thereby improving overall agent effectiveness and enabling contact centers to better support business goals.
3. **Compliance and risk management:** Ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements is crucial in industries from financial services to healthcare and beyond. Speech analytics helps monitor conversations for compliance breaches and mitigate potential risks by identifying non-compliant language and actions, allowing agents to correct mistakes before incurring penalties.
4. **Operational efficiency:** By identifying common issues and frequently asked questions with contact center speech analytics, businesses can streamline processes and reduce average handling time, leading to increased efficiency and reduced operational costs.

## The business impact of contact center speech analytics

By utilizing speech analytics in contact centers, businesses facilitate benefits that transcend functional improvements and directly impact the bottom line. These include:

### Improved customer insights

Contact center speech analytics allow businesses to gain a deeper understanding of customer needs and preferences, paving the way for more personalized and effective interactions.Considering that 79% of customers expect seamless and personalized experiences across channels, the value of deeper customer insights can’t be overstated.

### Enhanced decision-making

Data-driven insights from speech analytics enable better strategic decisions, such as product improvements and marketing strategies. Upwards of 90% of an organization’s data, including conversational data, is unstructured, meaning it holds immense but long untapped potential. With contact center speech analytics, businesses can finally unlock the full value of their customer interactions and harness it for more informed decision-making.

### Increased customer retention

Addressing customer issues proactively and improving service quality can lead to higher customer loyalty and retention rates. On top of this, better understanding customer interactions through contact center speech analytics can help businesses identify upselling and cross-selling opportunities, enabling them to capitalize on the revenue-generating potential of their service centers.

### Competitive advantage

Businesses that leverage contact center speech analytics can stay ahead of the competition by continuously refining their customer service strategies based on real-time insights. Rather than struggling with rigid and out-of-date processes, these up-to-date insights unlock true agility.

## How to choose the right solution

Because contact center speech analytics is foundational to unleashing the full potential of any contact center, there’s no shortage of solutions available today. But it’s important to approach vendors with a discerning eye; when looking for a solution, consider questions like:

1. How well does the solution integrate with your existing contact center infrastructure, such as your CRM and other critical systems?
2. Can the solution scale with your business needs, handling increasing volumes of calls without compromising performance?
3. Does the solution offer customization options to meet your specific business requirements? For example, if you work in a highly regulated industry, can the solution analyze calls based on tailored regulatory requirements?
4. Does the solution provide real-time analysis, post-call analysis, or both? If only post-call analysis is available, be mindful that this will significantly limit the benefits you can expect.
5. How advanced are the AI and machine learning capabilities of the solution? Do they provide actionable insights that will support your overall business goals?

Remember, not all tools are created equal. Carefully consider the value you’re hoping to find from a solution and evaluate options based on the vendors that best align with your goals.
