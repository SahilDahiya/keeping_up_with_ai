# ISO 27001, ISO 27701 and ISO 42001 Certification | Fireworks

AI adoption is accelerating, but enterprises face risk without verifiable security, privacy, and governance. Fireworks delivers on all three. We are proud to announce that Fireworks has achieved **ISO 27001, ISO 27701** and **ISO 42001** certifications, the leading global standards for information security, privacy management, and responsible AI governance. Few AI infrastructure providers hold all three, signaling a new benchmark in enterprise trust.

Enterprises are under increasing scrutiny as regulators move quickly: through 2025, all 50 U.S. states have introduced AI legislations or proposals ([NCSL](https://www.ncsl.org/technology-and-communication/artificial-intelligence-2025-legislation?)), highlighting that responsible, secure AI is no longer optional – it’s required. These certifications signal rigorous processes, independent audits, and continuous improvement, giving enterprises confidence that their data, models, and workflows are protected, and empowering them to own and govern their AI with confidence.

**What We Protect**

- **Customer Data**: Sensitive and regulated enterprise datasets across structured and unstructured formats
- **Models & Intellectual Property**: Fine-tuned models, proprietary workflows, and inference pipelines
- **Workflow & Integrations**: Enterprise processes, API calls, and system orchestration

**How We Protect it**

- **Certified Security Frameworks**: ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and ISO 42001 for security, privacy, and AI governance
- **Technical Controls:** Encryption at rest and in transit, access control with SSO/RBAC/2FA, audit trails, and monitoring
- **Governance & Compliance:** Internal audits, risk assessment, and alignment with global standards
- **Responsible AI Governance:** ISO 42001-certified practices formalize ethical, transparent, and risk-aware AI operations

**Why It Matters To You**

- **Confidence & Compliance:** Simplifies security reviews, audits, and procurement
- **Operational Safety:** Stronger safeguards across production AI and enterprise data environments
- **Control & Transparency:** Full ownership of models, data, and deployment, with visibility into all controls
- **Enterprise Differentiation:** Aligns with the highest standards for responsible AI, giving your AI initiatives a measurable edge

### Why This Matters to Enterprises

ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and ISO 42001 provide verifiable assurance that enterprise data, models, workflows, and AI practices are protected under recognized global standards. This is critical for regulated sectors such as finance, healthcare, and SaaS, where AI deployment depends on demonstrable security controls, traceability, and low operational risk.

For enterprises, the impact is direct:

- **Faster procurement and compliance cycles**: Streamlined audits and approval cycles
- **Stronger operational safeguards**: Reduced risk in production AI and sensitive data pipelines
- **Full Visibility & Control**: Transparency into all security, privacy, and risk posture
- **Responsible AI Alignment**: ISO 42001-certified practices are designed to ensure ethical, transparent, and accountable AI operations

These certifications differentiate Fireworks as one of the few AI infrastructure providers offering a comprehensive governance, security, and privacy framework. This reinforces our commitment to ethical, transparent, and risk-aware AI governance while giving enterprises full control over their models, data, and deployment.
