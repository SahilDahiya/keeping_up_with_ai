# Kyle Randolph joins Sierra as Chief Information Security Officer

We’re very excited to welcome our Chief Information Security Officer, [Kyle Randolph](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kylerandolph/).

Our customers trust us with their most valuable assets: their customers, their data, and their brand. As stewards of these assets, we take that responsibility seriously and hold ourselves to the highest standards of security.

Our strength in security is one of the reasons many of the world’s leading companies – including those in highly regulated industries and nearly half of the Fortune 50 – choose Sierra. Maintaining that trust requires us to keep raising the bar, especially as advances in AI introduce new security threats and opportunities.

Kyle brings 24 years of experience as a security leader and software engineer. He most recently served as CISO at Verkada and has built and led security teams and programs at Adobe, Citrix, Twitter, and Optimizely. His first-principles thinking, engineering mindset, and deep security expertise will help us build on that foundation.

Welcome to the Sierra team, Kyle!
